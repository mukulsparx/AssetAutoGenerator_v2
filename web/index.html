Asset Auto Generator v2

Version 2: Gemini scores every new version against the product photos (front, side and back) and, when Auto QA is on, accepts it or retries it for you. Each card shows the generated model with only the required steps. Tick Auto smooth (or other steps) if you want them in the saved GLB, then accept it, or fail it: the first retry runs on Meshy and starts as soon as you submit (with a short undo window). After a retry the card shows the earlier and the new version side by side, so you can keep either.
